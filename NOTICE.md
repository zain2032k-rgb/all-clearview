## Third party work

The recorded keyboard and mouse click sounds (Aula F75, FB67, 60he v2, Frosted 65 and the mouse click) in
`assets/clearview/keysamples` are taken from **SoberClient** by **Violet**, licensed under GPL-3.0-only.
The way a sample is chosen for each key (variant, pitch, space bar and stabilizer handling) follows the same
design as SoberClient's Creamy Keys module.
